Why take a short break?

Long stretches at a screen can leave you tired and slow. Many people find that a few minutes on something different, like a puzzle, helps them come back to work with a clearer head. These games are short on purpose: most rounds take under five minutes.

Free, private and nothing to install

  • No sign-up and no download. Open a game and play.
  • Nothing is saved. The games run in your browser. Your scores stay on the page until you close or reload it, and we never see them.
  • Works on phones. Every game works with touch, and you can also use a keyboard on a computer.

Frequently asked questions

Are these games free?

Yes. Every game is free to play, with no account, no download and no time limit on how often you play.

Do you save my scores?

No. We don't use cookies or browser storage, so your best score only lasts until you close or reload the page. If you want to keep a record, write it down or take a screenshot.

Who are these games for?

They are made for adults who want a short break from work or study, or who want to check their typing speed before a job application. They are not designed for young children.
