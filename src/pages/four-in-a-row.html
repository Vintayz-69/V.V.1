How to play

  1. Take turns dropping a counter into one of the seven columns. It falls to the lowest empty space.
  2. Get four in a row of your colour: across, up and down, or diagonally.
  3. Block your opponent when they have three in a row with a space to finish it.
  4. If the board fills up with no four in a row, the game is a draw.

How strong is the computer?

LevelHow it plays
EasyLooks at its own next move only, and about a third of the time plays a random column.
MediumLooks 3 moves ahead (its move, your reply, its next move).
HardLooks 6 moves ahead. It always takes a winning move and blocks yours when it sees one.

When it isn't sure, the computer prefers the middle columns, because a counter there can be part of more lines of four.

Worked example

There are 69 possible lines of four on a 7 × 6 board:

DirectionWorkingLines
Across4 starting places in each of 6 rows24
Up and down3 starting places in each of 7 columns21
Diagonal (each way)12 + 1224
Total24 + 21 + 2469

A counter in the bottom-middle space is part of 7 of those lines, while a corner counter is part of only 3. That's why the middle column is the strongest place to start.

Tips to win

  • Start in the middle column.
  • Make two threats at once. If you have two different places that would each finish four, your opponent can only block one.
  • Watch the space underneath. Don't drop a counter that lets your opponent play on top of it into a winning space.

Frequently asked questions

Can I play against a friend?

Yes. Choose "2 players" and take turns on the same phone or computer.

Is this Connect 4?

It's the same kind of game, often called four in a row. We wrote our own version from scratch, and it isn't connected with any other company's game.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes. Use Tab to reach the arrow buttons above each column and press Enter to drop a counter there.
