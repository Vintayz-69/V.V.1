Frequently asked questions

Are these calculators financial advice?

No. They do the maths for the numbers you type, which helps you plan and compare. Returns, interest rates and your own situation can change, so for decisions about investments, pensions or borrowing, talk to a qualified adviser.

Why does compound interest matter so much?

Interest is added to your balance, then the next interest is worked out on the bigger balance. Over many years that growth on growth can add up to much more than the money you put in. The compound interest calculator shows it year by year.
