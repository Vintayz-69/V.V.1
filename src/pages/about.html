How we build our tools

  • We show our working. Every calculator explains its formula and includes a worked example you can check by hand.
  • We check every number. Each calculator is tested against several examples before it goes live. Tax figures come from official government sources (IRS, HMRC, CRA and ATO) and are updated every tax year.
  • Your numbers stay with you. Calculations run in your browser. What you type into a calculator is never sent to us.
  • Your files stay with you. Our PDF tools work inside your browser. Files you open in them are never uploaded.
