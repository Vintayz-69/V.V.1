How TDEE is calculated

Your Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) is the total number of calories your body burns in a 24-hour day through cellular function, digestion, and movement.

Step 1: Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) via Mifflin-St Jeor: Men: BMR = (10 × weight_kg) + (6.25 × height_cm) - (5 × age) + 5 Women: BMR = (10 × weight_kg) + (6.25 × height_cm) - (5 × age) - 161 Step 2: Total Daily Expenditure: TDEE = BMR × Activity Multiplier

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between BMR and TDEE?

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the minimum energy required to keep vital organs functioning if you rested in bed all day. TDEE accounts for all physical activity, walking, workouts, and digestion on top of your BMR.

How large should a calorie deficit be for sustainable fat loss?

A 500 kcal daily deficit translates to approximately 3,500 kcal per week, resulting in roughly 0.5 kg (1 lb) of fat loss per week, which is considered safe, sustainable, and preserves muscle mass.

Is this medical advice?

No. This calculator provides mathematical estimations based on scientific population formulas. Consult a registered dietitian or medical professional before beginning any extreme dietary change.
