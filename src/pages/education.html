Frequently asked questions

Will my college or university calculate it the same way?

Not always. Grading scales, weightings and degree rules differ between schools, so use these tools as a guide and check your own course handbook or registrar for the exact rules.

Is my essay sent anywhere?

No. The word counter and text tools work inside your browser; nothing you paste is uploaded or saved.
