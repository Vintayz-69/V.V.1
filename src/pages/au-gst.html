Only charge GST if you're registered for it. You must register once your GST turnover reaches $75,000.

How it's calculated

Adding GST: GST = price × 10% total = price × 1.1 Removing GST: price = total ÷ 1.1 GST = total ÷ 11

A quick check: the GST in any GST-inclusive price is one-eleventh of that price.

A freelancer's invoice with a discount

Take the discount off first, then add GST to what's left. A designer quotes $2,000 for a logo and gives a 10% discount:

Price = $2,000.00 Discount 10% = −$200.00 Price before GST = $1,800.00 GST 10% = $180.00 Total to pay = $1,980.00

Adding GST first and then discounting gives the same total here, but the GST line on the invoice must match the discounted price. Our invoice generator applies the discount before tax, and can print "Tax invoice" at the top for GST-registered businesses.

A common mistake: dividing by 10

To find the GST inside a price that already includes it, divide by 11, not 10. GST is 10% of the price before GST, so it's one part in eleven of the total.

$1,100 including GST wrong: 1,100 ÷ 10 = $110 GST right: 1,100 ÷ 11 = $100 GST ($1,000 + $100 = $1,100)

A quick way to check: a $220 price includes $20 of GST, because $200 + 10% = $220.

Tips for freelancers' quotes and invoices

  • Say whether your price includes GST. "$1,000 + GST" and "$1,000 including GST" are $100 apart, and clients may read an unclear quote either way.
  • Work out GST on the invoice total as well as checking each line. Rounding each line to the cent can make the total a cent or two different.
  • Keep the GST apart from your income. If you're registered, the GST you charge isn't yours to keep, so don't count it when you work out what you've earned.

When to register for GST

  • You must register within 21 days once your GST turnover reaches, or is expected to reach, $75,000.
  • Taxi and ride-sourcing drivers must register whatever their turnover.
  • Below $75,000 you can choose to register, then you must charge GST and lodge activity statements.

What this calculator doesn't cover

  • GST-free sales (such as most basic food, some health and education) and input-taxed sales.
  • Exports and services to overseas customers, which are often GST-free.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work out GST from a total?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by 11. For example, a $220 invoice includes $20 of GST.

Do freelancers have to charge GST?

Only once you're registered. If your turnover is under $75,000 and you haven't registered, don't add GST to your invoices.

What's the difference between GST in Australia and other countries?

Australia's GST rate is 10%. Canada's GST/HST and the UK's VAT use different rates and rules. See our Canada GST/HST and UK VAT calculators.
