Only charge GST if you're registered for it. You must register once your GST turnover reaches $75,000.

How it's calculated

Adding GST: GST = price × 10% total = price × 1.1 Removing GST: price = total ÷ 1.1 GST = total ÷ 11

A quick check: the GST in any GST-inclusive price is one-eleventh of that price.

Worked examples

$1,000 excluding GST → GST $100 → $1,100 including GST $1,100 including GST → GST $100 → $1,000 excluding GST $550 including GST → GST $50 → $500 excluding GST

When to register for GST

  • You must register within 21 days once your GST turnover reaches, or is expected to reach, $75,000.
  • Taxi and ride-sourcing drivers must register whatever their turnover.
  • Below $75,000 you can choose to register, then you must charge GST and lodge activity statements.

What this calculator doesn't cover

  • GST-free sales (such as most basic food, some health and education) and input-taxed sales.
  • Exports and services to overseas customers, which are often GST-free.

Frequently asked questions

How do I work out GST from a total?

Divide the GST-inclusive price by 11. For example, a $220 invoice includes $20 of GST.

Do freelancers have to charge GST?

Only once you're registered. If your turnover is under $75,000 and you haven't registered, don't add GST to your invoices.
