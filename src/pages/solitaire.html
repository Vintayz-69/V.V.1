How to play Klondike Solitaire

  1. The goal: move all 52 cards onto the four foundations at the top right, one pile per suit, in order from Ace up to King.
  2. Build down on the seven piles: put a card on one that is one higher and the other colour, for example a red 6 on a black 7.
  3. Move runs of cards: a run that is already in order, alternating colours, can move together. When you move the last face-up card off a pile, the card underneath turns over.
  4. Empty piles take a King (or a run that starts with a King).
  5. Stuck? Tap the stock at the top left to turn over new cards. When it runs out, tap it again to turn the waste pile back over. You can go through it as many times as you like.

Worked example

Say the piles show a black 8 of clubs, a red 7 of hearts and a black 6 of spades on top, and an Ace of diamonds turns up from the stock.

  1. Tap the Ace of diamonds twice: it goes straight to an empty foundation.
  2. Tap the 7 of hearts, then the 8 of clubs: the red 7 goes on the black 8, and the card under the 7 turns over.
  3. Tap the 6 of spades, then the 7 of hearts: now that pile reads 8, 7, 6 in alternating colours, and the whole run can move together later.

Tips to win more often

  • Turn over hidden cards first. Moves that uncover a face-down card in the seven piles are usually worth more than moves from the stock.
  • Work on the biggest piles. The piles on the right hide the most cards.
  • Don't empty a pile without a King ready to go into the space.
  • Don't rush cards to the foundations. A low card in the right colour can be more useful in the piles, holding a place for other cards.

Frequently asked questions

Can every game be won?

No. The cards are shuffled at random, and some deals can't be finished whatever you do. If you are stuck, use Undo to try a different path or start a new game.

What's the difference between turning over 1 card and 3 cards?

With 1 card you see every card in the stock each time through, which makes the game easier. With 3 cards, only every third card is playable until you use the one on top, which is the classic and harder way to play.

Is my game saved?

No. We don't use cookies or browser storage, so the game is gone if you close or reload the page.

Can I play with a keyboard?

Yes. Every card you can move is a button: use Tab to reach it, Enter to pick it up, then Tab to the pile you want and press Enter again. Screen readers hear each card's name.
