Which calculator do I need?

Start from the question you're trying to answer:

  • "What should I charge?" The hourly rate calculator works back from the income you want, after time off, unpaid admin and tax. Contractors who charge by the day can use the day rate calculator, and writers and translators the per-word rate calculator.
  • "How do I price this project?" The project pricing calculator turns hours, costs and a safety buffer into a fixed price. To ask for a deposit and staged payments, use the milestone payment calculator. For ongoing work, the retainer calculator sets a monthly fee.
  • "Did that project pay enough?" The effective hourly rate calculator counts every hour, fee and cost. If your rate is too low, the rate increase calculator shows how much work you could lose after a price rise and still earn the same.
  • "How do I bill and get paid?" Make the bill with the invoice generator, check the date it's due with the invoice due date calculator, and if a UK business client pays late, work out what you can add with the late payment interest calculator.
  • "How much tax should I put aside?" The tax set-aside calculator gives an estimate for each payment in the US, UK, Canada or Australia, using official figures. For the full picture, open your country's tax tools above.
  • "My income goes up and down." The income smoothing calculator turns uneven months into a steady monthly pay, and the emergency fund calculator sizes a buffer for slow months.

Every calculator runs in your browser. What you type isn't sent to us or stored, and results are estimates to help you plan, not tax or financial advice.
