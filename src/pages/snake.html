How to play

  1. Press Start. The snake starts moving to the right.
  2. Steer with the arrow keys (or W A S D), by swiping on the board, or with the arrow buttons under it.
  3. Eat the food. Each piece scores 1 point and makes the snake one square longer.
  4. Don't crash into the walls or the snake's own body. Press Space or the Pause button to take a break.

The board and speeds

The board is 15 × 15 squares (225 in all), and the snake starts 3 squares long.

Worked example

The snake starts 3 squares long. After eating 10 pieces of food it is 3 + 10 = 13 squares long, and the score shows 10.

Tips for a longer snake

  • Stay near the edges early on and leave the middle open.
  • Move in wide loops as the snake grows, so your tail clears the way ahead.
  • Turning back on yourself is blocked, so a quick wrong key press won't end the game.
  • Start on Slow on a phone until swiping feels natural.

Frequently asked questions

Why doesn't the page scroll when I swipe on the board?

Swipes on the board steer the snake instead of moving the page. Swipe outside the board to scroll.

Can I go through the walls?

No. In this version, hitting a wall ends the game.

Is my score saved?

Only while the page is open. We don't use cookies or browser storage, so it's forgotten when you close or reload the page.
