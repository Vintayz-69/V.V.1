The main Self Assessment dates

For the 2026 to 2027 tax year (6 April 2026 to 5 April 2027), GOV.UK's deadlines are:

DeadlineWhat's due
5 October 2027Register for Self Assessment, if it's your first tax return or you didn't need to send one for 2025 to 2026
31 October 2027Paper tax return
30 December 2027Online return, if you want the tax collected through your tax code (you must already pay tax through PAYE and owe less than £3,000)
31 January 2028Online tax return, and pay the tax you owe (plus your first payment on account for 2027 to 2028, if you make them)

For the 2025 to 2026 tax year, each date is one year earlier: the online return and payment are due by 31 January 2027.

Payments on account

If you make payments on account, you pay towards next year's tax in two halves, by 31 January and 31 July. Each one is usually half of your last tax bill. Our payments on account calculator works out the amounts, including what you'll pay in January.

Making Tax Digital quarterly updates

If you use Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, you send an update after each quarter, and the deadlines are the same every year:

Update coversSend it by
6 April to 5 July7 August
6 April to 5 October7 November
6 April to 5 January7 February
6 April to 5 April7 May

Making Tax Digital started on 6 April 2026 for qualifying income over £50,000, and starts on 6 April 2027 for qualifying income over £30,000. GOV.UK says HMRC won't give penalty points for late quarterly updates in the 2026 to 2027 tax year.

How the calendar file works

The button makes a small calendar file (.ics) with one all-day event for each date in your list. It's made in your browser and saved straight to your device. We don't send it anywhere, and we can't see which dates you chose. Open the file to add the dates to your calendar, or import it in Google Calendar's settings.

What this page doesn't cover

  • VAT return deadlines, which depend on your VAT periods.
  • Company deadlines, such as Corporation Tax and Companies House filings.
  • Partnerships, trustees and non-resident companies, which can have different deadlines.
  • A late registration letter. If you register after 5 October, HMRC gives you a deadline 3 months from its letter or email, but you still need to pay by 31 January.

Frequently asked questions

When is the Self Assessment deadline for 2025 to 2026?

31 January 2027 for online returns and paying what you owe. The paper return deadline was 31 October 2026.

What happens if I miss the 31 January deadline?

GOV.UK says there's an initial £100 penalty, and more the later it is. Our self-employed tax page explains the penalties.

Do I get a reminder?

We don't send reminders or collect email addresses. Add the dates to your own calendar with the button above, and set reminders there.
