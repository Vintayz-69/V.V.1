Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a US gallon and an Imperial gallon?

A US liquid gallon is exactly 3.785411784 liters, whereas an Imperial gallon (historically used in the UK and Canada) is significantly larger at exactly 4.54609 liters (approx. 20% larger).

What is the base unit used for conversions?

All length measurements are mathematically normalized through the SI base meter (m), mass through the kilogram (kg), and volume through the liter (L) to ensure maximum floating-point precision.
