How the conversions work

Each unit is first changed into one base unit: metres for length, kilograms for weight, litres for volume and square metres for area. It's then changed from the base unit into the unit you want. Because every unit is linked to the same base, you can convert between any two units in a group, such as feet to miles or ounces to stone, without a separate table for each pair.

Temperature works differently, because the scales don't start at the same zero. Fahrenheit is converted with a formula rather than a single number to multiply by.

Many imperial and US units are defined exactly in metric terms. Since an international agreement in 1959, one inch is exactly 2.54 cm and one pound is exactly 0.45359237 kg, so these conversions are exact. Results are rounded to make them easier to read.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a US gallon and an imperial gallon?

A US gallon is exactly 3.785411784 litres. An imperial gallon, used in the UK, is exactly 4.54609 litres, about 20% bigger. Fuel economy in miles per gallon is not the same in the two countries for this reason. Our fuel cost calculator handles both.

Are US cups and fluid ounces the same as UK ones?

No. This converter uses US cups, pints and fluid ounces. UK (imperial) pints and fluid ounces are a different size, so a recipe from another country may need a different conversion.

Why is my result rounded?

Long decimals are hard to read and rarely needed. The calculation itself uses the full conversion factor, so rounding only affects how the answer is shown.
