Good to know

How we count

  • {{icon:calendar}}

    Whole calendar days

    Dates are counted as whole days, so daylight saving time changes never add or lose a day. Leap years are included.

  • {{icon:globe}}

    Your browser's time zone rules

    Time zones use the rules built into your browser, which your browser and device updates keep current.

  • {{icon:lock}}

    Nothing is sent

    Every answer is worked out on your own device. No sign-up, and nothing is saved.

Frequently asked questions

Do daylight saving changes affect the day counts?

No. Dates are counted as whole calendar days, so clock changes never add or lose a day. The time zone converter does include clock changes, using your browser's time zone rules.
