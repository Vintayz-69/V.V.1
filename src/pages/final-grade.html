How it's calculated

Course grade = grade so far × (1 − final's share) + final score × final's share Score needed = (grade wanted − grade so far × (1 − final's share)) ÷ final's share

Worked examples

78% so far, final worth 25%, want 80%: (80 − 78 × 0.75) ÷ 0.25 = (80 − 58.5) ÷ 0.25 = 86.0% needed 72% so far, final worth 40%, want 70%: (70 − 72 × 0.6) ÷ 0.4 = (70 − 43.2) ÷ 0.4 = 67.0% needed 85% so far, final worth 30%, want 90%: (90 − 85 × 0.7) ÷ 0.3 = (90 − 59.5) ÷ 0.3 = 101.7% needed (not reachable)

Frequently asked questions

What if my grade uses letters?

Use the percentage behind the letter. Your course syllabus says which percentages earn which letters, because each teacher or school sets their own scale.

What if the final is split into parts?

Add up the shares of every part still to come, and treat them as one final. The score you need is then the average you need across those parts.

How do I work out my overall GPA?

Once you have your course grades, our GPA calculator works out your semester or cumulative GPA on a 4.0 scale.
