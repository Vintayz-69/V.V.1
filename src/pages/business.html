Frequently asked questions

What's the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price; markup is profit as a share of the cost. A product that costs $60 and sells for $100 has a 40% margin but a 66.7% markup. The profit margin calculator shows both.

Do these tools give accounting or tax advice?

No. They do the maths for the numbers you enter. For tax, VAT registration or accounts, check the official guidance for your country or ask an accountant.
