The $30,000 small supplier test

Most businesses don't have to register for GST/HST while they're a small supplier. The CRA says you stop being one if your revenue from worldwide taxable supplies (before expenses) is more than $30,000 in either:

  • a single calendar quarter: you stop on the sale that takes you over $30,000, and you must charge GST/HST on that sale, or
  • the last four (or fewer) calendar quarters together: you stop at the end of the month after the quarter you went over, and start charging from your first sale after that.

Either way, you have to register within 29 days of your effective date of registration. Calendar quarters start in January, April, July and October. Include your associates' sales, but leave out financial services, sales of capital property and goodwill from selling a business.

The CRA's examples

Still a small supplier. Quarterly sales of $2,000, $10,000, $12,000 and $5,000 add up to $29,000 over four quarters, which isn't over $30,000.

Over in one quarter. $2,000, $10,000, then $38,000 in July to September 2020. The business stopped being a small supplier on the day it went over $30,000 in that quarter, and had to charge GST/HST on the sale that took it over.

Over in two quarters. A store opened in November 2019 and made $25,000 in October to December 2019 and $25,000 in January to March 2020: $50,000. It stopped being a small supplier on April 30, 2020, and its first sale after that was May 1, 2020.

Over four quarters. $2,000, $10,000, $12,000 and $8,000 from April 2019 to March 2020: $32,000. It also stopped on April 30, 2020.

The example above. $6,000 to $10,000 a quarter from October 2025. The four quarters to September 2026 make exactly $30,000, which isn't over. The four quarters to December 2026 make $34,000, so the business stops being a small supplier on January 31, 2027.

Once you're registered

You charge GST/HST on your sales and file returns. Some small businesses use the Quick Method to work out what they send to the CRA: our Quick Method calculator compares it with the regular method. To add GST/HST to a price, use the GST/HST calculator.

What this tracker doesn't cover

  • The exact day you go over in a single quarter. It tells you the quarter; you'll know from your own sales which sale it was.
  • Charities and public service bodies, which use different tests.
  • Registering voluntarily before you have to.
  • Quebec Sales Tax (QST), which Revenu Québec handles separately.

Frequently asked questions

Is the $30,000 threshold per year?

Not exactly. It's checked for each calendar quarter, and for the last four calendar quarters together. You can go over in any quarter.

Is it based on profit or sales?

Sales. The CRA says to include all revenue from taxable supplies before expenses.

How long do I have to register?

The CRA says within 29 days of your effective date of registration.
