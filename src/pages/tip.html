Frequently asked questions

Should you tip on pre-tax or post-tax totals?

Traditional etiquette suggests calculating the tip percentage on the subtotal before sales tax is added, though many electronic card payment terminals default to post-tax totals.

What should you do if an automatic service charge is already included?

If the receipt already itemizes a "Discretionary Service Charge" (common in London and high-end restaurants worldwide), you do not need to add an additional tip unless you choose to reward extraordinary service.
