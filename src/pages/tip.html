How the bill split is calculated

Tip Amount = Bill × (Tip % ÷ 100) Total Bill = Bill + Tip Amount Each Person = Total Bill ÷ Number of People

If you tick "round up", the total is rounded up to the next whole amount, and the extra goes to the tip. The calculator then shows the tip percentage you're really leaving.

Worked examples

$100.00 bill, 20% tip, 2 people tip 100 × 0.20 = $20.00; total $120.00; $60.00 each ($10.00 tip each) $85.50 bill, 18% tip, 3 people tip 85.50 × 0.18 = $15.39; total $100.89; $33.63 each $42.30 bill, 15% tip, 1 person, rounded up tip would be $6.35 → total rounded up to $49.00 tip becomes $6.70, which is 15.8% of the bill

When the total doesn't divide evenly, each share is rounded to the nearest cent, so the shares can add up to a cent or two more or less than the bill.

Before you tip: check the bill

  • Is a service charge already added? Some restaurants add one automatically, especially for large groups. It's often shown near the bottom of the bill. If it's there, the tip may already be included, so you don't have to add another one unless you want to.
  • Is the tip on the total before or after tax? Card machines sometimes suggest tips worked out on the total including sales tax. If you'd rather tip on the food and drinks only, enter the amount before tax here.
  • What's normal locally? Tipping customs differ between countries, and even between types of venue in the same city. We don't list "standard" percentages, because there's no official figure and customs change. If you're not sure, ask the staff or a local.

Frequently asked questions

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax total?

It's your choice. Tipping on the amount before tax gives a slightly smaller tip, because tax isn't part of the service. Enter whichever amount you want the percentage worked out on.

What if a service charge is already included?

Then the bill already includes a tip. Set the tip to 0% here to split the bill as it is, or add a small extra percentage if you want to leave more.

How do I split a bill when people ordered different amounts?

This calculator splits the bill evenly. If orders were very different, work out each person's food and drink total first, then run the calculator once for each person with "split between" set to 1, so everyone tips on what they ordered.
