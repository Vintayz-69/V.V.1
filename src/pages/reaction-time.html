    How to use the reaction time test

    1. Tap the box (or press Space) to start. It turns red and says "Wait for green".
    2. Wait. After a random pause of 1.5 to 4.5 seconds, the box turns green and says "Tap now!".
    3. Tap as fast as you can. Your time shows in milliseconds (ms). 1,000 ms is one second.
    4. Do it five times. You then get your average, best and slowest time.

    If you tap before the box turns green, that try doesn't count. Tap again to redo it.

    How your result is worked out

    Each try measures the time from the moment the box turns green to the moment your tap or key press reaches the page. Your result is the average of your five tries.

    Average = total of your 5 times ÷ 5

    Why your time can change

    • Your device. Screens, mice, keyboards and touch screens each add a small delay before the page sees your tap. A result on your phone and on your laptop may differ.
    • Tiredness and focus. Most people react more slowly when tired or distracted.
    • Practice. Your first few tries are often slower while you get used to the test.

    Frequently asked questions

    What is a good reaction time?

    The fairest comparison is with yourself. Because every device adds its own delay, comparing your number with someone on a different screen isn't exact. Take the test a few times on the same device and see whether your average improves.

    Is this a medical or driving test?

    No. It is a simple game for fun. It can't tell you whether you are fit to drive or work. If you are worried about your reactions, speak to a doctor.

    Why does the wait before green change each time?

    The pause is random, between 1.5 and 4.5 seconds, so you can't guess when to tap. You have to react to the colour change.

    Can I use it without a mouse?

    Yes. Use Tab to reach the box, then press Space or Enter to start and to react. The words in the box change as well as the colour.
