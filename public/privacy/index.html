Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026 · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information our games use, why, and the choices you have. It covers Dusk Ridge and every other game published by Vintayz Games Studio ("we", "us") on Google Play, unless a game has its own policy.

The short version

  • You can play without an account. Your progress is saved on your phone.
  • We do not run our own servers, and we do not sell your information.
  • If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google keeps your cloud save and ranking scores.
  • The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your phone's advertising ID. Buying No Ads stops AdMob from starting.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Friend Battles connect two nearby phones directly. Nothing is sent to us.
  • There is no chat, and we never read your contacts, camera, microphone or files.

1. Who we are

Vintayz Games Studio is an independent game developer based in India. We make games for Android phones and publish them on Google Play. For questions about this policy, write to us at the address in Contact us.

2. What is saved on your phone

Your game progress is stored only on your own phone. This includes your story levels, troops, coins, gems, skins, settings, the player name you type, and similar game data.

We cannot see this data. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data, unless you have turned on cloud save (see the next section).

3. Google Play Games (optional)

When you first open the game you can choose Google Play Games or Guest. You can change this later in Settings. If you sign in with Google Play Games:

  • Cloud save. A copy of your game progress is stored in your Google account, so you can carry it to a new phone.
  • Rankings and achievements. Your scores and achievements are sent to Google Play Games leaderboards. Other players can see your Play Games name, picture and scores there.
  • The game receives your Play Games player ID and display name from Google, only to run these features.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy. Guests use none of these features.

4. Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Most ads are optional videos you choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • your phone's advertising ID;
  • your approximate location, worked out from your IP address;
  • basic device information (model, Android version, language);
  • how you interact with ads (views and taps), and crash or diagnostic data from the ad software.

Google may use this to show ads that interest you. You can:

  • reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads or Settings → Privacy → Ads);
  • in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, give or refuse consent in the privacy message shown when the game starts;
  • buy No Ads. AdMob is then not started at all. We may still show small notes about our own games, which collect nothing.

Read more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and How Google uses advertising data.

5. Purchases

All real-money purchases (for example No Ads, gem and coin packs, and exclusive skins) are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment. We never receive your card, UPI or bank details.

The game receives a purchase receipt from Google (the product and a purchase token) so it can give you the item, and so a purchase is never given twice. Refunds are handled through Google Play.

6. Friend Battles and tournaments

Friend Battles and Friend Tournaments connect phones that are close to each other, using Google's Nearby Connections over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. The phones talk directly; nothing passes through us.

  • Your player name, chosen army, story level and the moves of the battle are shared with the other phones in the match.
  • Android requires a "nearby devices" or "location" permission to find nearby phones by Bluetooth. The game does not read, store or send your location. The permission is asked only when you open Friend Battles.

7. Player names, photos, sharing and reminders

Player name

The name you type on your Profile is shown to friends in Friend Battles and inside army codes you choose to share. A filter blocks rude words. Please do not use your full real name.

Photos

Photo mode saves a picture to your phone's Gallery only when you tap Save. If you tap Share, your phone's own share menu opens and you choose where it goes.

Invites and army codes

Invites and army codes are shared only when you choose to, through your phone's share menu.

Reminders

The game can show a reminder notification, for example when a free chest is ready. These are made on your phone; we do not send push messages from a server. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in Android settings.

Game engine

Our games are made with the Unity engine. Unity's own analytics and crash reporting are turned off in our games, but the engine may send basic technical information (such as device model and Android version) to Unity under the Unity Privacy Policy.

9. Who gets your information

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for money. Information goes only to:

  • Google, for ads (AdMob), Google Play Games, Google Play Billing and Nearby Connections;
  • Unity, for basic engine information, as described above;
  • other players, only the things described above (Play Games name and scores, or your player name in Friend Battles);
  • authorities, if the law requires it.

10. How long it is kept

  • On your phone: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Cloud save and rankings: kept by Google until you delete them or your Play Games account.
  • Ad data: kept by Google under its own retention rules.
  • Emails you send us: kept only as long as needed to answer you, and then deleted.

11. Deleting your data

  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Dusk Ridge → Storage → Clear data.
  • Cloud save, achievements and rankings: you can delete the data kept for our game, or your whole Play Games profile, in your Google Play Games settings.
  • Advertising ID: reset or delete it in your phone's settings.
  • Anything else: email us and we will help within 30 days. We hold no account of our own for you, so we may ask you to delete the items above yourself.

12. Children

Our games are made for players aged 13 and over, and they are rated as suitable for younger players too. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country, which is 18 for personal data under India's DPDP Act unless a parent agrees).

Children should play with a parent's permission. Parents can require approval for purchases with Google Play's parental controls and Google Family Link. If you believe a child has given personal information through our game, email us and we will help remove it.

13. Your rights by region

India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

You can ask for information about your personal data, ask for it to be corrected or erased, choose someone to act for you, and raise a complaint with us. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Europe, UK and Switzerland (GDPR and UK GDPR)

We rely on your consent for personalised ads (asked in the privacy message), and on performing our contract with you for running the game, cloud save and purchases. You can access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

United States (including California)

We do not sell personal information. Some laws treat ads based on your activity as "sharing". You can opt out by turning off ad personalisation or deleting your advertising ID in your phone's settings, or by buying No Ads. California residents can ask to know, correct or delete their personal information, and we will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Brazil (LGPD)

You can confirm whether we process your data, and ask for access, correction, anonymisation, portability or deletion, or withdraw consent.

To use any of these rights, email us. Because we do not keep accounts, most of your data is held on your phone or by Google, and we will point you to the right place or help directly.

14. Security and international transfers

Information sent to Google is encrypted in transit. Your phone's own protections keep your local save safe. Google may process data on servers in other countries, including the United States, with the legal safeguards described in its own policies.

15. Changes to this policy

If our games or the law change, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date. For important changes we will also tell you in the game.
