The 1099 thresholds for 2026

FormWho sends itWhen you get one for 2026
1099-NECA business client that paid you directly for your workIt paid you $2,000 or more in the year
1099-KA payment app or online marketplaceYou received more than $20,000 through it in more than 200 payments (both must be true)
1099-KA card processor (credit and debit card payments)Whatever the amount or number of payments

The IRS instructions say the 1099-NEC threshold was increased to $2,000 for tax years beginning after 2025, and may be adjusted for inflation from 2027. The IRS says the 1099-K threshold for payment apps and marketplaces went back to $20,000 and 200 transactions (IRS fact sheet FS-2026-07). Payments made by card or through a payment app go on a 1099-K, not a 1099-NEC, so you don't get two forms for the same money.

For payments made in 2026, clients must send you your 1099-NEC by January 31, 2027. That's a Sunday, so under the IRS weekend rule the deadline moves to February 1, 2027.

The most important rule: report all your income

The IRS says you must report all your income on your tax return, whether or not you get a Form 1099. The thresholds only decide which payers have to send a form. A client who paid you $1,800 doesn't send a 1099-NEC, but the $1,800 is still income. Keep your own records of every payment, such as invoices and bank statements, and add up your income from those, not from the forms.

How to use this checker

  1. List what each client paid you directly in 2026, one per line. You can type a name first, such as "Acme 2500", and only the number is used.
  2. Add payment app and marketplace income, and roughly how many separate payments it came in.
  3. Add card payments you took through your own card processor, if any.
  4. Add your business expenses to check whether you owe self-employment tax.

Worked examples

A freelancer with four clients. Clients paid $5,000, $2,500, $1,800 and $900 directly, plus $3,000 through a payment app in 40 payments. Two clients paid $2,000 or more, so expect two 1099-NECs totaling $7,500. The app total is under $20,000, so no 1099-K is required. Total income is $13,200, and $5,700 of it comes with no form but must still be reported. With $2,000 of expenses, profit is $11,200 and net earnings are $10,343.20, so self-employment tax applies.

An online seller under the transaction count. $25,000 of sales through a marketplace in 150 orders. That's over $20,000 but not over 200 transactions, so a 1099-K isn't required. All $25,000 is still income. With 250 orders, a 1099-K would be required.

Exactly $2,000. A client who paid you exactly $2,000 should send a 1099-NEC, because the rule is $2,000 or more.

Card payments. A tutor takes $4,000 in card payments through a card processor. The processor sends a 1099-K for any amount, so expect one form for the $4,000.

A very small side gig. One client paid $350 and there were no expenses. Net earnings are $350 × 92.35% = $323.23, under $400, so there's no self-employment tax. At $434 of profit, net earnings are $400.80 and it would apply.

Self-employment tax and the $400 rule

The IRS says you must pay self-employment tax and file Schedule SE if your net earnings from self-employment are $400 or more. Net earnings are 92.35% of your net profit (income minus business expenses). So the line is about $433 of profit. This is separate from whether you get any 1099s. What to do if a 1099 is wrong or missing

  • Missing form: report the income anyway, using your own records.
  • Wrong amount: ask the payer for a corrected form, and report what you actually received.
  • The same money on two forms: card and app payments belong on a 1099-K, not a 1099-NEC. Count the money once in your income, and keep records showing why.

What this checker doesn't cover

  • Payments to corporations. If your business is a corporation, or an LLC taxed as one, clients generally don't send you a 1099-NEC.
  • Other kinds of 1099, such as 1099-MISC for rent or royalties, 1099-INT for interest, or forms for backup withholding.
  • State taxes. It covers federal IRS forms only.
  • Whether you must file a tax return for other reasons. The $400 rule is only about self-employment tax.
  • Years other than 2026. The 1099-NEC figure may change for 2027.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to report income if I didn't get a 1099?

Yes. The IRS says you must report all income, whether or not you get a Form 1099-K or another information return. The same applies to a missing 1099-NEC.

What is the 1099-NEC threshold for 2026?

$2,000 or more paid to you by one client in the year. The IRS says it may be adjusted for inflation from 2027.

Will a payment app send me a 1099-K?

It must if you received more than $20,000 through it in more than 200 payments in the year. The IRS notes that some apps send forms for less.

When should I get my 1099s for 2026?

The deadline to send them is January 31, 2027, which moves to February 1, 2027 because January 31 is a Sunday.
