Delete your Dusk Ridge data

Dusk Ridge by Vintayz Games Studio · Last updated: 7 November 2026

Dusk Ridge has no accounts of its own and we run no servers. Your progress is saved on your phone, and, if you signed in, in Google Play Games. You can delete all of it yourself with the steps below, or email us and we will help within 30 days.

How to delete your data

1 Data on your phone

Your story progress, troops, coins, gems, skins, settings and player name.

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Tap Apps, then Dusk Ridge.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear data.

Uninstalling the game also deletes this data.

2 Cloud save, achievements and rankings

Only if you signed in with Google Play Games. This deletes your cloud save, achievements and ranking scores for Dusk Ridge.

  1. Open the Google Play Games app (or Play Store → Profile → Settings → Google Play Games).
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Play Games account & data.
  4. Under Delete individual game data, find Dusk Ridge and tap Delete.

You can also delete your whole Play Games profile on the same screen.

3 Advertising ID

Used by Google AdMob to show ads.

  1. Open your phone's Settings.
  2. Tap Google → Ads (on some phones Privacy → Ads).
  3. Tap Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).

4 Ask us

For anything else, or if you need help, email us. Please write "Dusk Ridge data deletion" in the subject. We will reply within 7 days and finish within 30 days.
