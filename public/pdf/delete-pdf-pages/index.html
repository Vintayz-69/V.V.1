Delete Pages from a PDF

Remove blank, extra or private pages from a PDF and save a new copy without them.

Your files never leave your device Free, no sign-up

or drop it here

    Your file stays on your device. It's edited by your browser and never uploaded.

    How to delete pages from a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF. You'll see a preview of every page.
    2. Pick the pages to remove. Tap them, or type their numbers (for example 2, 5-7). Tap a page again to keep it.
    3. Press "Delete pages" and download the new PDF. Your original file isn't changed.

    How it works

    The tool builds a new PDF from the pages you keep. The pages you delete are left out completely: their text and pictures aren't hidden somewhere inside the new file, they simply aren't in it. That matters when you're removing a page with private details before sharing a document.

    Kept pages are copied as they are, so their quality doesn't change. Everything happens in your browser, and your file is never uploaded.

    Example

    A 12-page contract where pages 5 and 11 are blank. Tap those two pages, or type 5, 11, and press Delete pages.

    12 pages − 2 deleted (5 and 11) = 10 pages Old pages 6–10 become new pages 5–9, and old page 12 becomes page 10.

    Frequently asked questions

    Will the file get smaller?

    Usually, yes, because the deleted pages' text and pictures are gone. How much smaller depends on what was on those pages.

    Can I get a deleted page back?

    Not from the new file, but your original PDF isn't changed. Keep it until you're happy with the new one.

    Are bookmarks and form fields kept?

    The pages you keep keep their text, pictures and web links. Bookmarks and fillable form fields aren't carried over, though anything already typed into a form still shows on the page.

    I want to keep just a few pages. Is there a quicker way?

    Yes. Use Extract pages and pick the pages you want to keep instead.
