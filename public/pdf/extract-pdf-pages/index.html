Extract Pages from a PDF

Pick the pages you need from a PDF and save just those pages as a new file.

Your files never leave your device Free, no sign-up

or drop it here

    Your file stays on your device. It's handled by your browser and never uploaded.

    How to extract pages from a PDF

    1. Choose your PDF. You'll see a preview of every page.
    2. Pick the pages you want. Tap them, or type their numbers (for example 2-4, 9).
    3. Press "Extract pages" and download your new PDF with just those pages.

    How it works

    The tool copies the pages you pick into a brand-new PDF, in the same order they're in now. Pages aren't re-saved, so text stays sharp and pictures keep their quality. The pages you don't pick aren't in the new file at all, and your original file isn't changed. It all happens in your browser, so your PDF is never uploaded.

    Example

    From a 20-page report, you only need the summary on pages 2 to 4 and the table on page 9. Type 2-4, 9.

    2-4, 9 → pages 2, 3, 4 and 9 = a new 4-page PDF Old page 9 becomes page 4 of the new file.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I change the order of the pages?

    Extracted pages keep their original order. To change it, extract them first, then use Rearrange PDF pages on the new file.

    How is this different from splitting a PDF?

    Extracting gives you one new file with the pages you pick. Splitting cuts a PDF into several files, for example one per chapter or one per page.

    Does extracting lower the quality?

    No. The pages are copied exactly as they are.

    Are links and form fields kept?

    Web links and links between the pages you keep still work. Bookmarks and fillable form fields aren't carried over, though anything already typed into a form still shows on the page.
