Rearrange PDF Pages

Put the pages of a PDF in a new order by dragging them or using the arrows, then save the PDF.

Your files never leave your device Free, no sign-up

    Your file stays on your device. It's handled by your browser and never uploaded.

    How to rearrange PDF pages

    1. Choose your PDF. You'll see a preview of every page, labelled with its current page number.
    2. Move the pages. On a computer, drag a page to its new place. On a phone, use the arrows under each page to move it earlier or later. "Reverse order" flips the whole document.
    3. Press "Save new order" and download the PDF.

    How it works

    A PDF keeps a list of its pages in reading order. This tool rewrites that list and nothing else. The pages themselves aren't copied or re-saved, so quality doesn't change, and bookmarks, links and form fields keep pointing at the right pages. Your file never leaves your device.

    Example

    You scanned a 4-page letter but fed it into the scanner last page first, so the PDF reads 4, 3, 2, 1. Press "Reverse order" and save.

    Before 4, 3, 2, 1 After 1, 2, 3, 4

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I move a page on my phone?

    Yes. Use the left and right arrows under a page. Each tap moves it one place earlier or later.

    Do bookmarks and links still work after rearranging?

    Yes. Only the order changes, so a bookmark or link that pointed to a page still takes you to that same page in its new position.

    Can I delete or rotate pages here too?

    This tool only changes the order. To remove pages, use Delete PDF pages; to turn them, use Rotate PDF.

    Can I put pages from different PDFs together?

    Yes, in two steps: merge the files first, then rearrange the pages of the merged PDF here.
