Frequently asked questions

Is it safe to paste code, keys or passwords here?

These tools run in your browser and send nothing to us, so what you paste stays on your device. Even so, it's good practice not to paste live secret keys into any website: use test values where you can.

Do the tools work offline?

Once a page has loaded, the tool itself works without the internet, because all the work happens in your browser.
