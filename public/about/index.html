About ToolNest

ToolNest makes free calculators for freelancers and self-employed people in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia. It also has everyday tools, such as PDF, image and text tools, that work without uploading your files.

Going freelance means answering money questions that employees never have to think about. How much should I charge? How much tax should I set aside? What happens in a quiet month? Each calculator gives an estimate in seconds and shows exactly how it was worked out, so you can check it yourself.

Who makes ToolNest

I'm Kunal Rajput, a student based in India. I build and look after this website myself: the calculators, the tests that check them, and the explanations on each page. ToolNest is part of Vintayz (vintayz.com).

ToolNest is independent. It isn't connected to any government, tax authority, bank or accounting firm.

I'm not an accountant or a financial adviser, and I don't present myself as one. That is why the tools work the way they do: every tax figure comes from the official government source and is linked on the page, every result is called an estimate, and each tax tool says what it doesn't cover.

How the tools are checked

  • Official figures only. Tax rates, thresholds and allowances come from the official sources: the IRS in the US, GOV.UK (HMRC) in the UK, the CRA in Canada and the ATO in Australia, plus legislation.gov.uk and the Bank of England for UK late payment interest. Each tax tool lists its sources and the date they were checked.
  • Examples worked out by hand. Every calculator is tested against at least three examples worked out by hand. More than 2,000 automated checks run before each change goes live.
  • The working is shown. Each calculator explains its formula in plain words, so you can see how a result was reached instead of trusting a black box.
  • Updated for each tax year. US and Canadian figures are updated in January, UK figures before 6 April and Australian figures before 1 July. If an update isn't ready in time, the page says which tax year its figures are for.

Your privacy

  • Your numbers stay with you. Calculations run in your browser. What you type into a calculator is never sent to us.
  • Your files stay with you. PDF and image tools work inside your browser. Files you open in them are never uploaded.
  • No cookies, no accounts. You don't need to sign up, and nothing is stored on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

What you'll find here

  • Freelance tools: hourly and day rates, project pricing, billable hours, invoices and emergency funds
  • Country tax tools for the US, UK, Canada and Australia
  • Business and career calculators, such as profit margin, break-even and salary to hourly
  • Resume and CV makers for each country, plus cover letter and resignation letter makers
  • PDF, image and text tools that never upload your files
  • A few short brain games for a break between tasks

Found a mistake?

Please tell me. Include the page link, the numbers you entered and the result you expected. I check the report against the official source, fix the tool if it's wrong, and update the "Last updated" date on the page. I read every message.

Important

The calculators give estimates to help you plan. They are not tax, legal or financial advice. For decisions about your own situation, please talk to a qualified accountant. See the disclaimer.
