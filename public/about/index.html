About ToolNest

ToolNest makes free, fast and accurate calculators for freelancers and self-employed people in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Australia.

Going freelance means answering money questions that employees never have to think about. How much should I charge? How much tax should I set aside? What happens in a quiet month? Our tools give you a clear answer in seconds, and show exactly how that answer was worked out.

How we build our tools

  • We show our working. Every calculator explains its formula and includes a worked example you can check by hand.
  • We check every number. Each calculator is tested against several examples before it goes live. Tax figures come from official government sources (IRS, HMRC, CRA and ATO) and are updated every tax year.
  • Your numbers stay with you. Calculations run in your browser. What you type into a calculator is never sent to us.

Important

Our calculators give estimates to help you plan. They are not tax, legal or financial advice. For decisions about your own situation, please talk to a qualified accountant. See our disclaimer.
