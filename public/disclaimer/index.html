Accuracy

We work hard to keep our calculators accurate and up to date. Tax figures are based on official government sources, and each page shows when it was last updated. However, we can't guarantee that every result is complete, correct or current for your situation.

PDF tools

Our PDF tools change files on your own device. We test them carefully, but PDF files vary a lot and a tool may not handle every file perfectly. For example, bookmarks and fillable form fields are not kept when you merge, split or remove pages. Always keep your original file and check the new one before you use it.

Our Sign PDF tool adds a picture of your signature to a page. It is not a certified digital signature, and we can't say whether a particular person or organisation will accept it. If it matters, ask whoever asked you to sign.

Image, text and date tools

Our image tools make a new copy of your picture on your own device; always keep your original and check the new picture before you use it. Our text tools change and compare text by fixed rules, so check the result, especially names and anything legal or official. Our date and time tools use your device's calendar and the time zone rules built into your browser; contracts, laws and official deadlines can count days differently, so check the wording of any rule that applies to you.

Games

Our games are for fun. Typing speeds and reaction times include delays from your own screen, keyboard, mouse or touch screen, so they are a rough guide only. They are not a medical, driving or job test.
