How to convert salary to an hourly wage

  1. Determine your annual hours: Multiply the hours you work each week by the number of weeks you are paid per year. For a full-time job (40 hours/week, 52 paid weeks), this equals 2,080 hours per year.
  2. Divide your annual salary by annual hours: This reveals your unadjusted baseline hourly wage.
  3. Factor in overtime or extra hours: If you regularly put in unpaid overtime (e.g. 50 hours/week on a salaried exempt contract), your real hourly compensation drops significantly.

How it's calculated

Annual Working Hours = Paid Weeks/Year × Hours Worked/Week Hourly Rate = Annual Salary ÷ Annual Working Hours Weekly Pay = Hourly Rate × Hours Worked/Week Bi-Weekly Pay = Weekly Pay × 2 Daily Pay = Weekly Pay ÷ Days Worked/Week Monthly Pay = Annual Salary ÷ 12 Overtime Rate (1.5x) = Hourly Rate × 1.5

Worked example

Jordan earns an annual salary of $65,000.00 in the US, working 40 hours per week across 5 days, with 52 paid weeks (including PTO). Jordan's annual hours are 2,080. Dividing $65,000 by 2,080 yields an equivalent hourly rate of $31.25 per hour. Jordan earns $250.00 per day, $1,250.00 per week, and receives a bi-weekly paycheck of $2,500.00 before tax deductions. If eligible for time-and-a-half overtime, the overtime rate is $46.88/hour.

Frequently asked questions

How many working hours are in a year?

In standard full-time employment (40 hours per week for 52 weeks), there are 2,080 working hours in a year. At 37.5 hours per week, a common full-time contract in the UK, it's 1,950 hours.

Does this calculation include taxes?

No. It shows your gross (before-tax) pay. Tax depends on your country and situation. If you are self-employed, our tax calculators estimate what you keep after tax in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

What if I take unpaid time off?

If you take 2 weeks of unpaid leave, change "Paid Weeks Per Year" to 50. This recalculates your true hourly rate based on the actual hours you spent earning that annual total.
