Frequently asked questions

Are the tax figures up to date?

Country pay tools use figures from official sources (IRS, HMRC, CRA and ATO) for the tax year shown on each page, stored in one place and checked before each new tax year. Your own payslip can differ, for example because of tax codes, benefits or pension choices.

Should I compare jobs on salary alone?

Salary is only part of it. Hours, commuting time and cost, bonuses and benefits all change what a job pays per hour of your time, which is what the job offer comparison works out.
