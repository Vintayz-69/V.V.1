How FICA payroll tax is deducted

Every W-2 salaried employee in the United States pays mandatory FICA (Federal Insurance Contributions Act) payroll taxes, matched by their employer:

  • Social Security: 6.2% on gross earnings up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500. Wages above this limit are exempt from Social Security tax.
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all earnings with no cap. High earners pay an Additional Medicare tax of 0.9% on wages over $200,000 (single) or $250,000 (married filing jointly).

Frequently asked questions

How do 401(k) contributions reduce my federal taxes?

Pre-tax contributions to a traditional 401(k) or 403(b) retirement account are subtracted from your gross wages before federal and state income tax brackets are applied, immediately reducing your taxable income.

Which US states have 0% personal income tax?

As of 2026, Alaska, Florida, Nevada, New Hampshire, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Washington, and Wyoming levy no state income tax on earned wages.
