How to use the sales tax calculator

  1. Choose "Add tax" to go from a price to the total, or "Remove tax" to find the price before tax from a receipt total.
  2. Type the amount and your sales tax rate. In the US the rate is usually the state rate plus any county and city rates, so check your state's department of revenue for the rate where the sale happens.
  3. Read the tax and the total.

How it's calculated

Add tax tax = price × rate ÷ 100 total = price + tax Remove tax price = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100) tax = total − price

To remove tax, divide; don't subtract the rate from the total. Taking 8.25% off a total that includes 8.25% tax gives too little, because the tax was worked out on the smaller, pre-tax price.

Worked examples

The rates below are only examples, not the rate anywhere in particular.

Three everyday sums the calculator helps with

1. Setting a price so the total is a round number

Say you sell at a market and want customers to pay exactly $50.00 including 7% tax. Choose "Remove tax" and type 50 and 7:

Price before tax = 50 ÷ 1.07 = $46.73 Tax = 50 − 46.73 = $3.27

So your price tag is $46.73 before tax. The 7% here is only an example rate.

2. Finding the rate from a receipt

If a receipt shows the price and the tax, the rate is tax ÷ price × 100. A receipt with $80.00 of goods and $5.60 tax used a rate of 5.60 ÷ 80 × 100 = 7%. Because tax is rounded to the cent, small amounts can give a slightly odd rate, so use the biggest receipt you have.

3. Why tax per item and tax on the total can differ

Three items at $0.99 each, at 8.25%:

Tax on each item = 0.99 × 8.25% = $0.08, × 3 items = $0.24 Tax on the total = 2.97 × 8.25% = $0.25

Both are rounded correctly, but they're a cent apart. Which one a shop uses depends on its till and the state's rules, so a one-cent difference on a receipt isn't usually a mistake.

Sales tax, VAT and GST: what's the difference?

US sales tax is usually added at the till on top of the shelf price, and the rate depends on where the sale happens. The UK, Canada and Australia use a national VAT, GST or HST instead, with set national rates and their own rules for businesses that register to charge it. Frequently asked questions

Why don't you fill in my state's rate?

US sales tax rates depend on the state, county, city and sometimes special districts, and they change. Your state's department of revenue publishes the rate for each address. Typing your own rate keeps the answer right for where you are.

Do I charge sales tax as a freelancer?

It depends on what you sell and where. In many US states services aren't taxed but some are, and the rules for digital products differ again. Check your state's department of revenue, or ask an accountant.

Why is my receipt a cent different?

Shops round tax per item or per receipt, and the rule differs by state. We round the final tax to the nearest cent. See the $0.99 example above.

How do I add sales tax in my head?

Find 10% by moving the decimal point one place, then adjust. For 8% of $45: 10% is $4.50, 1% is $0.45, so 8% is 4.50 − 0.90 = $3.60. The calculator is quicker for odd rates such as 8.25%.

Is the rate the same across a whole state?

Often not. Many states let counties and cities add their own rate on top of the state rate, so two shops in the same state can charge different totals. Your state's department of revenue publishes the rates.
